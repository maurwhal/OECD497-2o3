Deferred

SARA-ICE

SARA-ICE's ED01/POD/GHS-category output comes from a proprietary Bayesian model (NICEATM/Unilever) calibrated on 400+ ICE chemicals — the coefficients aren't published, so it can't be faithfully reproduced client-side here. Rather than approximate it and risk a wrong number, use the official tool directly: NTP SARA-ICE webapp. A future version of this toolkit may add a helper that formats your entered data into the exact SARA-ICE input template (Substances/HPPT/LLNA/DPRA/kDPRA/KeratinoSens/h-CLAT/U-SENS tabs) for one-click upload there.

All calculators run entirely client-side — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Provided for informational purposes; final scientific interpretation remains the responsibility of the user.
