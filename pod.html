Results — models trained on LLNA data (EC3)

pED = model's linear predictor; EC3% = MW / 10^pED; DSA = EC3% × 250 (dose per skin area, µg/cm²).

Results — models trained on extended Potency Values (PV)

Per ESM3's own guidance: a conservative assessment may take the lower PoD of the LLNA- and PV-trained models, unless there's a mechanistic reason to prefer one (e.g. a strongly cytotoxic chemical with weak alerts/reactivity — prefer the PV-trained model there to avoid a cytotoxicity-driven bias).

Self-check against the ESM3 worked examples

Loads four worked examples internally (two with complete data, two with deliberately partial data) and compares this page's live output to values independently computed from the ESM3 formulas. If anything shows FAIL, do not trust this calculator's numbers until it's fixed.
